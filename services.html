What I’d do in your first 30 days

Every account is different, so this is the starting shape. We adjust it together in week one.

  1. Week 1

    Learn the brand

    I get access, read the last 90 days of posts and insights, and agree with you on goals, voice and what is off-limits.

  2. Week 2

    Plan and start posting

    A content calendar built on three or four pillars, the first Reels and graphics go out, and a routine for replying to comments and messages.

  3. Week 3

    Build the habits

    Daily replies, prompts that pull comments and tags, and two or three formats tested against each other. If there is a budget, I boost the strongest post.

  4. Week 4

    Report and adjust

    A plain summary of what the insights say, what to keep and what to drop, and the plan for the next month.

How we work together

Five steps, repeated every month.

  1. 01

    Audit

    I look at your page, your last 90 days of insights and your inbox to see what already works.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We agree on content pillars, posting days and a SMART goal with KPIs, then I build the month’s calendar.

  3. 03

    Create

    I shoot or edit Reels, design graphics and write captions, and send them for your approval.

  4. 04

    Engage

    I post, reply to comments and messages, and run events or giveaways for the community.

  5. 05

    Review

    At month end you get a short report: top posts, reach, replies, and what we change next.

FAQ

Questions clients usually ask.

Something else on your mind? Ask me by email.

Which platforms do you manage?

Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts and Discord. Most of my results so far come from Facebook Reels, TikTok videos and Discord communities.

Do you create the content, or only post it?

Both. I edit Reels and Shorts in CapCut, design graphics in Photoshop, Illustrator or Canva, write the captions, and then post and schedule them.

Can you set up and run a Discord server?

Yes. For DDTank Haven I designed the channel layout, set up reaction roles and a custom bot with MEE6, and organized events and giveaways.

Do you have social media certifications?

Yes. I’m a Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate: I passed Meta’s 100-101 exam on November 9, 2026 with a perfect score of 1000 out of 1000. I also completed five Meta courses on Coursera, from Introduction to Social Media Marketing to Measure and Optimize Social Media Marketing Campaigns, plus Google’s Foundations of Digital Marketing and E-commerce, and Udemy courses in social media marketing, AI video and web development. Every certificate on this page links to its verification page.

Can you run Facebook ads?

Yes. I’m Meta Certified in buying ads on Facebook, Instagram and Messenger. I boost Friesta’s strongest Reels, and I can plan full campaigns from a creative brief: audience, offer, budget, SMART goal and KPIs, then the ad creative and copy.

How do you use AI in your work?

I use Claude, ChatGPT Codex, Gemini and Meta AI for planning, first drafts of captions and ideas, and AI-assisted post designs such as the Friesta design library. I review and edit everything myself before it is published.

Are the numbers on this page real?

Yes. They come from Meta Business Suite and TikTok Studio, and the date range is shown next to each one. You can open the original screenshots on this page.

How do we get started?

Send me your page or server link, your goals, and what has been hard to keep up with. I will reply with what I would change first and how I would plan the first month.
