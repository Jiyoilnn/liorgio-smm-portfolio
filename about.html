About & experience

Four-plus years of posts, replies and community work.

I started by designing posts and answering customer messages for a retail shop in Las Piñas, and kept that page running for more than four years. Then I managed a gaming community across Discord, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. Today I run Friesta’s Facebook and TikTok, where short, relatable videos do most of the work.

In November 2026 I became a Meta Certified Digital Marketing Associate, passing Meta’s exam with a perfect score of 1000/1000. Before the exam I completed five Meta courses and Google’s Foundations of Digital Marketing and E-commerce on Coursera. Before that I trained in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign and took virtual assistant courses. I use AI tools to plan, draft and design, and I check every post before it goes out.
