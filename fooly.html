FOOLY

A 15-second vertical ad for FOOLY functional tea, made for the FOOLY team’s creative challenge.

The video

Two versions of the same 15-second ad, both 1080 × 1920 for Reels and TikTok.

What it covers

The on-screen text, in the order it appears.

  1. 0:00Hungry?Hook
  2. 0:01FOOLY tea helps you feel full in just 5–10 minMain benefit
  3. 0:04Only 25 caloriesLow calorie
  4. 0:06No artificial flavorsClean label
  5. 0:08Pick your flavor: Lemon, Mango, PeachAll three flavors
  6. 0:12Try FOOLY at DrinkFooly.comCall to action

How I made it

AI does the first draft. I direct it, check it and finish it.

  1. Step 1

    Brief to prompt

    I turned the brief into a shot-by-shot prompt for a 15-second, 9:16 ad: fast cuts, bright sunny light, and FOOLY’s turquoise, lemon, mango and peach palette.

  2. Step 2

    Generate in Google Flow

    I generated the scenes in Google Flow (Omni 1.2 Flash), with FOOLY’s product photos as reference images so the can matches the brand.

  3. Step 3

    Edit and caption

    I edited the clips, added captions in FOOLY’s colors, and exported a captioned version and a clean version without text.
